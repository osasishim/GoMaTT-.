The Heritage Living Room

A living room that tells a Nigerian story through craft, with bronze portrait busts, carved timber and warm daylight.

Start a similar project

About this project

Black-framed glass shelving displays bronze busts and carved panels against a calm ivory and cocoa backdrop. A low charcoal sofa with a timber base, a black marble coffee table and beaded ottomans keep the room grounded, open and easy to move through.

Design highlights

  • Display wall of bronze busts and a sculptural curved horn
  • Beaded ottomans inspired by Benin coral beads
  • Neutral wool rug to soften the hard materials
  • Slatted timber column framing the entrance
